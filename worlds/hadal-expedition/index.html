    Surface to 600 m

    Up to 40 m long. Not one animal: a colony of clones, each doing one job.

    100 to 4,500 m

    Females about 18 cm. The glowing lure is full of bacteria.

    Below 8,000 m

    Over 3 cm. Collected at 10,897 m. Digests sunken wood into sugar.

Where the numbers come from

  • Challenger Deep 10,935 ± 6 m: Greenaway et al., 2021. Everest 8,848.86 m: 2020 survey.
  • Light in water and red animals: NOAA Ocean Exploration.
  • Three in four animals glow: Martini and Haddock, Scientific Reports, 2017.
  • Atolla, Praya, Melanocetus: MBARI Deep-Sea Guide, WHOI.
  • Deepest fish filmed, 8,336 m: University of Western Australia, 2023.
  • Cuvier's beaked whale, 2,992 m and 137.5 minutes: Schorr et al., PLOS ONE, 2014.
  • Hirondellea gigas and wood: Kobayashi et al., PLOS ONE, 2012.
